What Cedal Star does

We keep it focused: Android, security, and the web. No fluff.

App Creation

Custom Android apps and tools, from launchers to utilities. We build from scratch for your idea or improve what you already have.

Security & “Hacking”

Security-focused features, app-lock systems, and basic testing. We think like attackers so we can design better defenses.

Websites & Dashboards

Simple, fast sites and admin panels for apps or brands. From landing pages to small dashboards connected to your backend.

AI & Automation

Voice assistants, simple bots, and automations around your app or site. We integrate existing AI services into your stack.

Debugging & Decoding

Fixing broken flows, tracking weird bugs, reading and explaining logs or code. If it's confusing, we help decode it.

Consult & Support

Need advice on how to build your idea, what stack to pick, or how to secure it? Cedal Star can guide and then build.
